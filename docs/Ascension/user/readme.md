# User Documentation,

Welcome to the **Ascension User Documentation** center.

Ascension is an intelligent climbing coach application that brings high-level biomechanical coaching directly to your smartphone. Whether you are an indoor boulderer, a sport climber, or a climbing gym coach, Ascension helps you analyze your climbing technique, identify movement inefficiencies, and progress faster with objective, AI-driven guidance.

:::info
**Language Standard:** The user documentation is written **entirely in English** to support climbers, gym partners, and coaches worldwide.
:::

---

## Core Capabilities

Ascension equips climbers with next-generation coaching tools right from their mobile device:

- **Climbing Sessions:** Start a session when you arrive at the gym so every climb analysis is automatically grouped together, giving you total duration and aggregated session statistics when you finish.
- **Objective Pose Analysis:** Film any climb from your smartphone. The AI analyzes your posture, movement cadence, balance, and center-of-mass trajectory frame by frame (2D pose first, with interactive 3D pose reconstruction arriving in a later milestone).
- **2D & Interactive 3D Skeleton Playback:** Visualize your 2D movement reconstruction over your video, and explore 3D reconstructions inside an interactive 3D scene (orbit, zoom, limb inspection).
- **Ghost Mode:** Photograph a route wall, select a detected hold group/color or choose individual holds manually, and receive an AI-computed step-by-step image sequence showing a feasible beta adapted to your morphology.
- **Same-Wall Climb Comparison:** Share public or friends-only analyses and compare your climbing technique side by side with another user who analyzed the same wall.
- **Adaptive Morphological Profiling:** Configure height, wingspan, weight, and declare injured or missing limbs so that movement recommendations adapt precisely to your physical reality.
- **Targeted Training Routines:** Receive customized training programs designed around your grade progression, historical weaknesses, and recovery goals.

- Creating an account and choosing your home gym or climbing discipline.

- Declaring injuries or limb limitations on the interactive skeleton for adapted feedback.

### 2\. Climbing Sessions & Filming Best Practices

- Starting and ending a gym climbing session to track workout duration and aggregated stats.
- Camera positioning, distance, and tripod recommendations.
- Wall lighting and framing guidelines to maximize hold and body detection accuracy.
- Video duration, format requirements, and direct upload workflow.

### 3\. Understanding Analysis Feedback

- Reading your overall climb score and movement efficiency rating.
- Interpreting joint-by-joint biomechanical metrics (elbow flare, hip distance to wall, dynamic momentum).
- Reviewing actionable technique tips generated by the AI coaching assistant and comparing runs with friends on the same wall.

### 4\. Master the Ghost Mode

- Photographing a route and choosing detected hold groups (by color/route) or choosing holds manually.
- Computing a feasible climbing beta tailored to your body proportions.
- Navigating through the step-by-step move images to decode tricky route crux sequences before climbing.

### 5\. Training Routines & Progression Tracking

- Building and logging personalized warm-up, strength, and endurance routines.
- Tracking technical improvements over weeks and months.
- Sharing ascents and climbing sessions with friends.

### 6\. Subscription Plans & Quotas

- Overview of tiers: **Freemium** (free starter), **Premium** (€20/month), and **Infinity** (€30/month).
- Quota management: monthly video analyses, Ghost Mode executions, and custom routine limits.

### 7\. FAQ & Troubleshooting

- Common questions regarding video processing times, device compatibility, and offline usage.
- Contacting Ascension support and submitting community feedback.
